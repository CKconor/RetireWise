# RetireWise

A personal UK retirement planner. This glossary covers the language used to answer "have I got enough, what if something goes wrong, and am I saving too hard?"

## Language

**Enough**:
The smallest pot at retirement that funds retirement spending to the end of the plan on expected returns, allowing for tax and state pension. It has two levels: Essentials Enough and Lifestyle Enough.
_Avoid_: Target, goal, number

**Essentials**:
Retirement spending the person could not reasonably go without, after tax and in today's money.
_Avoid_: Basics, needs

**Nice-to-haves**:
Retirement spending on top of Essentials, after tax and in today's money. Essentials plus Nice-to-haves is the person's full lifestyle.
_Avoid_: Wants, discretionary

**Living costs**:
What must still be paid each month today if income stopped. Unlike Essentials, this describes life now, not retirement.
_Avoid_: Monthly spending, expenses

**Named scenario**:
A specific, describable bad outcome a plan is tested against on its own: Crash, Poor returns or Living to 98.
_Avoid_: Stress test, percentile, simulation

**Verdict**:
The honest pass or fail of a plan on expected returns and against each Named scenario, at each level of Enough. A level fails if its spending can't be paid in full in any single year.
_Avoid_: Confidence score, success rate, peace of mind score

**Job-loss drill**:
A worked example of losing work: how long Emergency cash lasts and what pausing contributions costs the plan.
_Avoid_: Fire drill, stress test

**Emergency cash**:
Money set aside to be spent straight away in a crisis. It counts towards net worth but not towards the retirement pot.
_Avoid_: Rainy-day fund, safety buffer

**Over-saving**:
The amount a person could stop saving each month, spend now, or the years they could retire sooner, while the plan stays on a chosen line.
_Avoid_: Surplus, safety buffer

**Secure line**:
The point where Essentials pass every case of the Verdict and Lifestyle passes on expected returns.

**Strict line**:
The point where both Essentials and Lifestyle pass every case of the Verdict.
